<p class="eyebrow">Nature Communications · 2020</p>
<h1>Satellite imagery &amp; economic well-being</h1>
<p class="page-intro">Using publicly available satellite imagery and deep learning to understand economic well-being in Africa</p>
<p>Christopher Yeh, Anthony Perez, Anne Driscoll, George Azzari, <strong>Zhongyi Tang</strong>, David Lobell, Stefano Ermon, and Marshall Burke.</p>
<p>We combine deep learning with public daytime and nighttime satellite imagery to predict local asset wealth across sub-Saharan Africa. Trained on survey-based wealth measures, the models are evaluated on unseen locations and on changes in wealth over time.</p>
